# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a respectful place for everyone who takes part: reporting an issue, sending a change, or answering a question.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Keep to the topic. Give feedback that helps the other person act on it.
- Accept that maintainers decide what goes into the project, and that their time is limited.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language, in any form.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, advertising unrelated to the project, or deliberately wasting maintainers' time.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, use [GitHub's private report on the repository](https://github.com/miniwall-app/miniwall-android/security/advisories/new) and say it is a conduct report, or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately.

This text is short on purpose. It applies on GitHub and in any space where you speak for the project.
